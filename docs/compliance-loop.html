Inside the Compliance Loop

The loop answers two questions about the operated system: does it meet the Article 22 epfd(down) limits, latitude by latitude — and if not, what is the smallest GSO-exclusion discipline that does? Whatever it finds is written back into the operation profile, so the declaration and the flown behaviour stay one thing.

The loop, end to end

The sweep

A victim earth station is planted at each latitude of a grid (default 0–70° in 10° steps) at your chosen ES longitude, with the wanted GSO satellite at a fixed longitude offset and an S.1428 dish. Each victim gets the same truth run the Simulation runner performs — the live scheduled beam composition, or, when the profile declares the mask footprint source, the examination's own read of the declared PFD mask (Recommendation ITU-R S.1503-4 §D5.1.4.1). Each latitude becomes one row: the maximum epfd seen, the verdict, and the worst dB margin. Export table… writes the rows as CSV.

Tech · verdict and margin

The verdict is the examination's own comparison: at every limit point, the simulated level exceeded for the allowed percentage of time must not pass the limit level (S.1503-4 §D7.1.3, over the §D7.1.2 CDF bins). The margin at one point is the limit epfd minus the measured epfd at that point's percentage; the row reports the minimum over the points — positive means room to spare, and the worst row is the binding latitude.

The limits

The sweep verdicts against the limit points in the text box — one epfd_db percent line per point. Type them from the applicable Article 22 table, or load them: point at the BR limits database and Load reads the rows applicable to the profile's downlink carrier and the shells' lowest operating height; pick a row and Use fills the text. Each row names its reference bandwidth and its own reference dish diameter — set the victim dish to the row's diameter, since that is the antenna the limit was written for. Latitude-dependent short-term rows cannot fit the flat text and are shown for hand transcription of your latitude band.

The advisor

Suggest exclusion walks the global exclusion angle from the profile's current value upward in your step, re-running the full sweep at each value, and stops at the first fully compliant angle. The walk is deliberately linear, not a bisection, because the walk itself is an output: every intermediate sweep is a point on the margin-versus-alpha trajectory, and the failure report uses it — the worst margin at the end, the latitude holding it, and whether it was improving, worsening or not moving with alpha. From the trend it says what to do next: raise the cap and keep walking, accept that a larger exclusion worsens this geometry, or recognise that the exclusion is not the binding lever and the system itself must change.

Apply to profile then writes the found angle into the profile file. That is the loop's invariant: the advisor's exclusion becomes scheduler-enforced operation, never an advisory note — otherwise the projection margin between mask and truth would measure a fiction. When only some latitudes failed at the start, the report also notes that per-latitude alpha rows would be the finer declaration (an open item, tracked with the scene-gating work it depends on).

Tech · the power headroom line

epfd moves exactly dB-for-dB with the per-beam transmit power density — the payload envelope study's linear frontier — so every sweep's worst margin doubles as the TxEirpDbw headroom: how much per-beam power the payload may add (or must shed) at the swept exclusion. The summary prints it after every sweep and at the advisor's endpoints. Live-composition footprint only: a declared mask is a fixed file, and a power change would need the mask regenerated.

Reading the depth honestly

The defaults — 0.1 day at 60 s, 144 steps per sweep — are screening-grade, chosen so a fifteen-step walk stays affordable. Short-term limit points at very small percentages need far longer runs to even resolve: a point at 0.017% of the time is decided by a handful of deepest samples, on the order of six thousand steps or more. The loop's job at screening depth is to locate the compliant region, the binding latitude and the trend; the final word belongs to a long run at the found angle. The window also warns when the profile carries per-latitude exclusion rows the payload's single scene ring cannot express — a verdict computed in that state names its gap rather than hiding it.
